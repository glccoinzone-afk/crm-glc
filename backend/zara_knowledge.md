## CAREER OPPORTUNITIES (job, franchise, DPCP)
- Anyone asking about a job, work, earning, franchise, agency, dealership, or "DPCP" should be sent to https://glczone.in/careers . The page explains each option in detail and has a simple application form. There is also a "Career Opportunities" tab on every GLC Zone portal page.
- There are exactly three options: (1) Get a Job, (2) Become a Franchise (GLC Fresh or GLC Grocery), (3) DPCP Partner.
- Applications are only taken through the form on the page. After the form, the GLC Zone team calls the person back. For a job, the team reviews the qualification and invites the person for an interview. There is no direct login or panel for a job: the interview comes first.
- Do NOT promise or guess any salary, income, commission figure beyond what is written here, or a joining date. Earnings depend on work and no income is guaranteed.
- Never invent contact details (email, LinkedIn, Instagram or phone numbers). The only contacts you may give are: the website glczone.in, the careers page glczone.in/careers, support@glczone.in, and this WhatsApp number. If someone wants a person, say the team will call them back after they fill the form.

### Get a Job
- Roles depend on qualification and interest: delivery, billing / POS, office, customer support. The role is decided in the interview.
- A delivery rider earns 75% of the delivery charge of every delivered parcel and gets a rider ID card.
- Form asks: name, mobile, city, qualification, experience (optional), email (optional).

### Become a Franchise (GLC Fresh / GLC Grocery)
- The franchise handles and buys the goods in its city. Selling, pricing, delivery and profit or loss belong to the company. MRP, selling price and SPV are controlled only by the company.
- Commission is on the purchase value: GLC Fresh 8%; GLC Grocery 4% to 8% (the company fixes the rate for each purchase). Paid within 7 working days with a statement.
- Refundable deposit (returned within 30 days when the agreement ends): GLC Fresh vegetables Rs 20,000, fruits Rs 20,000, both Rs 40,000; GLC Grocery Rs 50,000. Yearly software fee Rs 2,000, not refundable.
- The company provides goods, packing material, transport and delivery, branding and advertising, one trained employee (salary paid by the company) and the franchise certificate.
- The franchise provides a shop or space, counter and bill machine, electricity, internet, computer and printer, crates, weighing scale, water, packing staff, security/CCTV, furniture and a rubber stamp.

### DPCP Partner (Delivery Payment Channel Partner)
- A DPCP manages the riders of an area (district or block). Riders collect Cash on Delivery (COD); the cash comes to the DPCP, who checks the accounts (daily Day Close) and passes it to the company on time.
- Commission is a % of the month's COD volume (returned items are deducted). The % is fixed in the agreement. The previous month's commission is paid into the bank the next month with a UTR number.
- Needed: an area, riders (or the ability to bring them), KYC (ID, PAN, bank), a security deposit and cash limit set in the agreement. Any cash shortage is the DPCP's responsibility. A DPCP is an independent partner, not an employee, with no fixed salary.
- A DPCP gets a portal (installs like an app) with riders, cash, handovers, Day Close, commission statements and chat. A district DPCP can create block agents.

## GLC GROCERY (kirana / staples) - delivery rules
- Minimum order Rs 199. Delivery charge: Rs 30 for orders from Rs 199 up to Rs 499.99; Rs 20 for orders of Rs 500 and more. Above 15 kg, Rs 2 per kg extra. There is always a delivery charge (no free delivery).
- GLC Fresh (fruits and vegetables) delivery: Rs 20 flat up to 15 kg, then Rs 2 per kg.
- Fresh and Grocery orders in one cart become separate orders, each with its own delivery charge.
